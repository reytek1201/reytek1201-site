Channel guide

The bottom of the phone becomes a HUD readout instead of a tab bar. It shows the channel you're on, a live signal meter and one key. The key opens a full-screen guide over the vortex.

  1. Tap the key (or the readout) to open the guide.
  2. Pick a channel. The page tunes in, and the strip updates.
  3. Turn on Now playing below to see the Phase 4 state: a spinning record and the track in the strip, and the meter moves to the music.
