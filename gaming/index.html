› Player one · how I got here
  1. 1984

    The arcades

    My dad's arcades were my first playground. Galaga taught me patterns. Donkey Kong taught me patience.

  2. 1986

    Nintendo

    The arcade came home. I had one the year it came out, and the TV was never mine again.

  3. 1989

    Sega Genesis

    Then the console wars started. Sega brought the speed, and I picked a side every week.

  4. 1995

    PlayStation

    Day one. Discs instead of cartridges, worlds in 3D instead of sprites.

  5. 1998

    PC

    Keyboard and mouse took over. It's where I play now, and where POE Source lives.
